# Dead simple Permissions for Laravel

Deadbolt was intended to provide really, really simple permissions for Laravel when the bigger roles and permissions
packages felt like overkill. There are plenty of projects I worked on where things like Spatie's `laravel-permission`
package were just to much. Deadbolt made implementing a simple permissions system really easy.

I've even used Deadbolt in larger, more complex projects without problems.

Complete roles and permissions can be important in many projects, but for those situations where it can feel like
overkill, Deadbolt fits really well.

Deadbolt hasn't seen much work recently and isn't actively maintained. Although I do use it in some places, It really
just gets the occational dependency update.
