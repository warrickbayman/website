# Online Workout and Fitness Platform

When Donna's Health and Fitness decided that they wanted to expand their physical gym offering to a
wider, digital audience, they started to make use of the MediaPortal platform. However, the system wasn't
really designed for their business model, and it didn't take long to realize that they needed something
a little more built-for-purpose.

The Donna's Health and Fitness online gym is designed to lead users through a large library of workouts
spread across various categories. Donna was able to come up with a brand-new set of plans exclusively
for online users.

## Consolidated Payments

In addition, existing physical gym members were moved into a special category of users so that they
would have access to basic technique videos, and the client was able to consolidate their payment platform
across all users. No matter if your an online exclusive user, a physical gym member, or both, everyone
pays for their access to the gym in the same way.

## QEncode to Vimeo

Videos on Donna's online gym were originally transcoded using QEncode and stored locally.
However, after some discussion a recent update moved the entire catalogue onto Vimeo.
