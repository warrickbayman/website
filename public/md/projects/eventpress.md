# End-to-End Event Management

EventPress is probably the project I work on the most and spend at least half my work week on this one
application. It is the existence of EventPress that has allowed me to work for myself.

EventPress was started in 2017 in response to a simple request from a subsidiary of one of South Africa's
largest financial institutions (from here on, referred to as "The Bank"). That request was to develop a
simple RSVP management tool that could replace their reliance on the Excel Spreadsheets they had been using.

The goalposts moved many times and the simple RSVP management tool grew from a small, simple job, into
an almost 10 year development journey.

## Deploying to Enterprise

EventPress lead me down some completely new paths, but possibly the biggest challenge of my career was
deploying what we had built to an enterprise environment. The Bank that had started our journey now
wanted to make EventPress their ONLY event management platform.

We were required to interface with their 2 factor authentication system through LDAP, and would be required
to make use of their centralized Microsoft SQL Server. Up until this point, my only experience with
relational databases was MySQL.

There were many roadblocks along the way. One of the biggest was convincing the technology powers at The
Bank that an application written in PHP was good enough to be run in an enterprise environment.

Today, EventPress is central to all of The Banks events. We use QR codes to manage check-in and
registration and guest itineraries, we allow users to download guest lists, add custom RSVP fields and
we have a fairly robust "logic" system we built for our forms.

I'm very proud of where EventPress has ended up and I believe that there is a lot of journey to go.
